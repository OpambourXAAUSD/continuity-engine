# Sample as-built (sample-site-north, SAMPLE ONLY)

This is an invented sample as-built for the competition demo. It is not a real site document.

- Sample power meter shows zero: check the sample meter gateway link first. Do not unplug sample meters.
- Sample gateway is connected through the sample Ignition service. A service restart can drop the sample gateway until the service is restarted.
- Sample pump is connected to the sample pump starter panel. Trip history is recorded at that panel.

- Sample UPS is connected to the sample battery bank. Sample UPS battery voltage is recorded at the sample UPS panel.
- Sample AHU fan is connected to the sample AHU starter panel.
- Sample generator is connected to the sample generator control panel. Sample generator fuel level is recorded there.
- Sample CRAH unit is connected to the sample chilled water loop.
- Sample network switch is in the sample network rack. Sample network switch port status is shown there.
