# Sample Sequence of Operations (sample-site-north, SAMPLE ONLY)

This is an invented sample SOO for the competition demo. It is not a real site document.

- Sample gateway offline after service restart: first remote check is restart the sample Ignition service, then confirm the sample gateway trend. Do not power-cycle sample meters or gateways.
- Sample pump trip alarm repeating: check sample pump starter and trip history first. Do not reset the sample pump repeatedly without checking the trip history.
- Sample chilled water loop setpoint is 7 degrees C. If the loop is above setpoint, check the sample chiller, not the sample gateway.

- Sample UPS battery alarm low: first check sample UPS battery voltage. Do not reset sample UPS without checking battery voltage.
- Sample air handling unit fan fault: check sample AHU fan starter and airflow sensor first.
- Sample chilled water pump cavitation noise: check sample chilled water pump inlet pressure first. Do not run sample pump dry.
- Sample fire alarm panel trouble: check sample fire panel trouble log first. Do not silence sample fire alarm without logging it.
- Sample generator test failure: check sample generator battery and fuel level first.
- Sample data hall temperature high: check sample CRAH fan speed and chilled water valve first.
- Sample network switch link down: check sample network switch port status and cable first.
- Sample EPMS trend gap: check sample EPMS data collector service first. Do not restart sample meters for a trend gap.
- Sample lighting panel breaker trip: check sample lighting panel load first.
